# GridMind AI — Phase 2 Data Model Specification

## 1. Overview & Disclaimer

This document defines the data models, entity relationships, physical units, and JSON payload contracts for the **GridMind AI Phase 2 Synthetic Factory Data Engine**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SYNTHETIC SIMULATION DISCLAIMER**  
> All data structures, load curves, equipment power ratings, generation availability curves, and anomaly profiles generated by this engine are **purely synthetic assumptions** created for software prototyping and renewable energy optimisation algorithms.  
> They do not represent measured SCADA, IoT, or smart-meter telemetry from any real-world industrial installation.

---

## 2. The Virtual Factory: Profile & Technical Specifications

The simulated entity is **PrecisionWorks Factory**, a medium-sized precision automotive machining and component fabrication factory located in the Fictional Demonstration Facility (Synthetic Profile).

### 2.1. Physical Units & Conventions
| Quantity | Unit | Symbol | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Instantaneous Power** | Kilowatt | `kW` | Electric power demand or generation rate |
| **Energy Consumption / Generation** | Kilowatt-hour | `kWh` | For 1-hour interval, $10\text{ kW} \times 1\text{ h} = 10\text{ kWh}$ |
| **Tariff / Electricity Cost** | Indian Rupee | `INR` (₹) | Expressed as numeric float per kWh (e.g. `8.20`) |
| **Production Output** | Components / Units | `units` | Finished precision parts machined per hour |
| **Timestamps** | ISO 8601 | — | Indian Standard Time (`+05:30`, e.g. `2026-10-14T08:00:00+05:30`) |
| **Interval Duration** | Hours | `h` | Explicitly `1.0` |

### 2.2. Production Zones & Connected Equipment
- **Total Connected Factory Rating**: `260.0 kW`
- **Facility Baseload**: `10.0 kW` constant (lighting, HVAC, servers, compressed air dryer standby).
- **Production Zones**:
  1. **Zone 1: CNC Machining & Milling (`zone_cnc`)**
     - *Rated Capacity*: 120.0 kW | *Production Capacity*: 120 units/hour
     - *Vertical Machining Center 1 (`CNC_VMC_01`)*: Rated 45.0 kW | Active: 18.0–26.0 kW | Idle: 3.5 kW
     - *Vertical Machining Center 2 (`CNC_VMC_02`)*: Rated 45.0 kW | Active: 18.0–26.0 kW | Idle: 3.5 kW
     - *Industrial Air Compressor (`COMP_AIR_01`)*: Rated 30.0 kW | Active: 14.0–20.0 kW | Idle: 4.0 kW
  2. **Zone 2: Induction Heat Treatment & Annealing (`zone_heat_treatment`)**
     - *Rated Capacity*: 90.0 kW | *Production Capacity*: 80 units/hour
     - *Induction Hardening Furnace (`IND_FURNACE_01`)*: Rated 75.0 kW | Active: 35.0–50.0 kW | Idle: 5.0 kW
     - *Quench Circulation Pumps (`QUENCH_PUMP_01`)*: Rated 15.0 kW | Active: 6.0–10.0 kW | Idle: 1.5 kW
  3. **Zone 3: Finishing, Inspection & Packaging (`zone_finishing_packaging`)**
     - *Rated Capacity*: 40.0 kW | *Production Capacity*: 100 units/hour
     - *Surface Grinding & Deburring (`GRIND_DEBURR_01`)*: Rated 25.0 kW | Active: 8.0–14.0 kW | Idle: 2.0 kW
     - *Automated Inspection & Boxing (`INSPECT_PACK_01`)*: Rated 15.0 kW | Active: 4.0–8.0 kW | Idle: 1.0 kW

### 2.3. Production Operating Schedule
- **Shift 1: Morning Primary (08:00 – 16:00)**: All 3 zones active; 100% production target.
- **Shift 2: Evening Secondary (16:00 – 24:00)**: Zones 1 & 3 active; Zone 2 heat treatment curtailed during peak tariff window.
- **Shift 3: Night Maintenance / Off-Shift (00:00 – 08:00)**: Production paused; machines in low standby/idle.

### 2.4. Generation & Grid Energy Assets
- **Captive Solar PV**: 100 kWp rooftop monocrystalline array. Operates 06:00–18:00, peaking at midday (~85 kW). Zero at night.
- **Captive Wind Turbine**: 60 kW direct-drive turbine. Diurnal generation based on typical inland wind curves (15–45 kW).
- **Biomass Gasifier / Generator**: 50 kW dispatchable plant with 250 kg daily agro-waste briquettes (cotton stalk / bagasse, ~312.5 kWh daily limit, ₹4.20/kWh fuel cost).
- **Grid Electricity**: 250 kW contract demand sanctioned load under Regional Utility HT-industrial Time-of-Day (ToD) tariff:
  - *Off-Peak / Night (22:00 – 06:00)*: ₹6.50 / kWh
  - *Normal / Day (06:00 – 18:00)*: ₹8.20 / kWh
  - *Peak Evening Surcharge (18:00 – 22:00)*: ₹11.80 / kWh
- **Battery Energy Storage System (BESS - Future Allocation Phase)**: 100 kWh capacity, 50 kW max power, 90% roundtrip efficiency.
